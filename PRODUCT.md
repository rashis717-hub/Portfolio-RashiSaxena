## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, design and product leads, and recruiters evaluating Rashi Saxena, a UX Lead in enterprise and agentic AI product design. They skim first, then read one case study deeply.

## Product Purpose

A custom portfolio that shows depth of judgement, not just screens. Success means a visitor knows who Rashi is within seconds, can scan every case study quickly, and can read one in depth without friction. The site must be fast and accessible.

## Positioning

Undecided beyond the brief: the portfolio of a UX Lead whose work is enterprise and agentic AI product design, presented through the reasoning behind decisions rather than visuals alone.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive to evaluate a candidate, typically on a laptop or phone, often between other tasks. They skim the home page, pick one case study and read it closely. Case studies follow a numbered-section structure with stakeholder cards and key metrics.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js (App Router, static export), Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, MDX case studies with validated frontmatter. See AGENTS.md.
- Case studies are added by writing an MDX file plus images; no component changes.
- Some case studies may be private or redacted (frontmatter `visibility` and `redact`).
- No content, numbers, client names or quotes may be invented; missing facts use `[YOUR INPUT]` placeholders.

## Brand Commitments

- Personality: calm, precise, editorial. The work leads; the interface stays quiet.
- Visual authority is /DESIGN.md ("Quiet Precision"). Impeccable advises; DESIGN.md decides (see .agents/rules/impeccable.md).
- Positive references: a case-study structure with numbered sections, stakeholder cards and big metrics; a landing page with one statement, a numbered work list and a contact line.
- Anti-references: generic AI-gradient SaaS landing pages, neon or glassmorphism overload, stock illustrations, emoji in the UI, and any photograph of Rashi (none is used; the identity is typographic).
- Voice: plain, specific, first person. No filler such as "passionate" or "innovative".

## Product Principles

1. Judgement over decoration: every case study explains decisions, trade-offs and outcomes.
2. Skimmable first, deep second: structure each page so a skim and a close read both work.
3. Truth only: real facts, real numbers, or a visible placeholder.
4. Fast and accessible by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, visible keyboard focus, full keyboard operation, semantic HTML, and a reduced-motion version of every animation.
