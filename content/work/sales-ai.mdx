<Section n="01" title="Context & Business Problem" headline="Every sales AI tool solved one problem and added one more dashboard">

### The landscape

Sales organisations are being offered AI tools from every direction. One listens to meetings. One reads the health of the sales pipeline. One helps plan actions in a market. One helps prepare a pitch. Each is useful. Together they are four logins, four ways of navigating, and four different ideas of what a sales manager needs.

This work was a demonstration, built with BCG, of what it looks like when those tools stop being separate apps. The company in the demo is a fictional waterproofing-chemicals business called Bondex, so the data is safe to show and the story is easy to follow.

### The four tools

| Tool | What it does | Built by |
|---|---|---|
| Huddle.AI | Meeting intelligence: turns meetings into insights, actions and scores. This is SalesPulze. | Covasant |
| Insights.AI | A sales thermometer: the health of sales activity at a glance | Covasant |
| MAP.AI | Market Action Planner: plans actions for a market | BCG |
| Pitch.AI | A pitch agent: helps prepare a pitch | BCG |

A fifth tool, Churn.AI, was considered and then dropped from the scope.

### The core problem

- **Four tools, four mental models.** Each one organises information differently.
- **The person is lost between them.** A sales manager's day does not follow the boundaries between tools.
- **Signals do not travel.** What one tool learns does not reach the next.
- **A demo of four dashboards proves nothing.** It shows four products. It does not show a better way to work.

| What people experience | What it costs the business |
|---|---|
| Four apps for one job | Switching, missed signals and low adoption |
| Dashboards built around the tool, not the person | Managers search for what is theirs |
| Insights that stay in their own tool | Good signals never reach the decision |

### The idea

Start from the person, not the tool. A single layer reads who is signed in and shows the signals, tools and actions that matter for that role, drawing on all four tools underneath.

### What shaped the design

1. **Persona first, tool second.** People think in jobs, not in products.
2. **Signals should travel.** What one tool learns should be visible where the next decision is made.
3. **One layer, one design language.** The seams between tools, and between two companies, should not show.

### Four tools, and with one layer

This comparison shows what the design is built to change. These are design intentions, not measured results.

| Dimension | Four separate tools | One persona-based layer |
|---|---|---|
| Where a person starts | Which app to open | Their own role |
| Moving between tools | Switching and re-orienting | One navigation and one language |
| Signals | Stay in their own tool | Travel to the next decision |
| Look and feel | Four styles | One design system |
