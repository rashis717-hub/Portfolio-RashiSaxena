<Section number="01" label="The Challenge" title="Governing agents from draft to production across isolated enterprise tenants">

As organizations deploy multiple specialized AI agents, managing their lifecycles becomes a major operational bottleneck. Teams build agents in isolation, struggle to track prompt revisions and tool permissions, and lack structured promotion gates between development and production.

For Zebra, the goal was establishing a central Agent Registry capable of serving forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) operating across isolated tenant environments. Every agent needed auditable versioning, explicit promotion gates across Dev, Staging, UAT, and Production, and safe deployment rollback capabilities.

</Section>
